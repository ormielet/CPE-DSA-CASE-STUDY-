Case Study Information

THIS SECTION Explains....

Parking Garage

Accept a car using its plate number. Enter the plate and choose arrival or departure. The program shows how many times each car arrived and departed. The garage holds only 10 cars.

Stack LIFO

Only the top car can leave. Cars above a departing car move out temporarily, then return in their original order.

Queue FIFO

Cars enter at the rear and leave from the front. Cars ahead of a departing car re-queue at the rear.

Tree

Trees organize data in levels, with each node linking to the nodes below it. These two modules look at how a tree is built and how it is searched.
